Where we're headed in 2027

Diaspora Peoples Place began in early 2024, when our community was in emergency fundraising mode for Gaza. At one benefit concert, the performers, vendors and service providers who gave their time all were unpaid and increasingly unable to pay their own bills. Backstage, many told us they would need day jobs or quit creative work altogether. That's when we realized our advocacy is only as strong as our financial stability as a community. So we built The Peoples Marketplace to do it differently: everyone is paid fairly, and the proceeds fund micro-grants for creatives and entrepreneurs, the people who preserve and carry our heritage.

More than 4,000 of you have gathered with us since 2024. Together we've given out 130 free booths and $4,500+ in micro-grants from our Empowerment Fund to artists and entrepreneurs.

And this spring, NOCHELLA 2026 raised more than $23,800 for families in Gaza: $11,990 for Anera and $11,850 for HEAL Palestine.

In 2027, NOCHELLA returns in the spring and The Peoples Marketplace becomes a fall festival. We're finalizing the details and can't wait to share. The Solidarity Edit comes back for Eid and Black Friday, and our Empowerment Fund is aiming for at least ten micro-grants.

We're breaking up with social media

Platforms censor and surveil us while profiting from our culture and creativity. So we're spending less time on social media and more in the spaces we hold together: your inbox, our website and our gatherings.

You can now find us on Substack and in our WhatsApp community for updates between gatherings.
