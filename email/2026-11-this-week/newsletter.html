Hi friends,

Our community keeps growing, so here's a quick reintroduction. Diaspora Peoples Place keeps diaspora cultures alive in the West by supporting our makers, bringing our communities together, and uniting our movements.

It started in early 2024, when the artists, photographers, sound engineers, and vendors who power our events and connect us to our cultures were working benefit after benefit for little or nothing. Our advocacy is only as strong as our financial stability as a community, so we built gatherings where everyone gets paid. Since then, 6,000+ of you have gathered with us, and we've given 185 free booths and $4,571 in micro-grants to creatives and small businesses.

When our makers close their businesses, our movements lose the music, the craftsmanship, the recipes, the films, and the anthems that keep us connected to our causes. Community is everything, and it starts with showing up for each other. Here's where to start this week.
